About the Clinic

Full-Cycle Dentistry in Odesa

MaslovClinic is a full-cycle dental clinic on Arkadiivske Plato in Odesa. We specialize in dental implants, full-mouth rehabilitation, ceramic veneers and crowns, as well as restorative and surgical treatment.

Clinic Specialties

Cosmetic dentistry, dental implants, periodontics, orthodontics, endodontic treatment (root canal therapy), teeth whitening, and professional oral hygiene — see the full list of specialties and prices on the "Services" page.

Dentistry in Odesa Near the Sea

The clinic is located in the Prymorskyi district of Odesa, on Arkadiivske Plato, 5A, Bldg 1 — just 100 meters from the sea, with easy access from the city center and Arkadia. Panoramic windows, a spacious lobby, and a well-planned layout of rooms create a calm setting for patients of any age.

Implant Treatment in Odesa

We work with three implant systems — Nobel Biocare, Straumann and JDental — from single-tooth implants to full rehabilitation under All-on-4/6 protocols. For more on pricing and protocols, see the "Implant Treatment" page.

Family Dentistry in the Prymorskyi District

We treat patients of all ages — from routine check-ups and hygiene to complex prosthetics. See our doctors and their specialties on the "Our Doctors" page, and real treatment results in the "Our Cases" section.

Why Choose MaslovClinic

Our own 3D diagnostic room (CBCT), a dedicated sterilization unit with Tecno-Gaz autoclaves, and work with premium implant systems from Nobel Biocare, Straumann and JDental — three protocols to fit different patient budgets.

Free parking, and a comfortable location in the Prymorskyi district of Odesa — 100 meters from the sea, with easy access from the city center and Arkadia.
