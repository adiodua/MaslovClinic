CT and Panoramic Dental X-Rays:
When They're Needed and What They Show

Both scans show your teeth and jaws, but for different purposes — here's how they differ, when each is needed, whether it's safe, and what diagnostics cost in Odesa.

Book a Visit
MaslovClinic · Blog

A panoramic X-ray and a CT scan aren't the same thing

Both give a picture of your teeth and jaws, just in different ways. A panoramic X-ray (orthopantomogram) is a flat, two-dimensional image of both jaws taken in a few seconds and used as a quick overview tool. A CT (computed tomography) scan builds a full 3D model — letting the dentist examine a root, a canal, or a section of bone from any angle, not just one projection.

When a panoramic X-ray is enough

A panoramic X-ray is usually enough for a routine check-up, a general assessment of teeth and gums, follow-up after cavity treatment, and an initial look at wisdom teeth — when you just need to see whether they're there and how they're positioned.

When you actually need a CT scan

A CT scan is ordered when an exact 3D picture matters: before implant placement, to assess bone volume and density; before a complex or surgical wisdom tooth extraction, to see its position relative to the nerve; before root canal treatment, if a regular X-ray doesn't give a clear picture; or when a hidden pocket of inflammation is suspected that isn't visible in two dimensions.

Who shouldn't get a CT scan

The main restriction is pregnancy, since the method relies on X-ray radiation. Otherwise, modern equipment delivers a low radiation dose, comparable to a few hours of everyday background exposure, so CT is considered safe for most patients, including children, when a dentist orders it.

What the appointment looks like

The dentist first reviews your concerns before you're even in the chair, to plan out exactly which images are needed. Next comes an exam, followed by a targeted X-ray, a panoramic X-ray, a CT scan, or a scan — whichever fits your specific case. Afterward, the dentist explains the results and lays out treatment options with pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a dental CT scan cost in Odesa?

The price depends on the scan's scope — a segment, one jaw, or both. A consultation and basic X-rays at MaslovClinic start from 400 UAH; the dentist gives an exact CT price once the needed scope is determined.

Who shouldn't get a dental CT scan?

The main contraindication is pregnancy. Otherwise, modern equipment delivers a low radiation dose and is considered safe for most patients.

How is a panoramic X-ray different from a CT scan?

A panoramic X-ray is a flat 2D image of both jaws for a quick overview; a CT scan is a full 3D model for precisely assessing roots, canals, and bone.

Do you need a CT scan before implant placement?

Yes, a CT scan is a standard part of implant planning: it shows bone volume and density, which the dentist uses to plan the implant placement.
