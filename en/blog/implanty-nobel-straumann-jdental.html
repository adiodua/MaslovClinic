Straumann & JDental: What's the Difference

Three implant systems — three different budgets and clinical scenarios. Here's what actually sets them apart, not just the price.

MaslovClinic · Blog

Why You Even Need to Choose Between Systems

An implant isn't just "a screw in the bone." The manufacturer determines the thread shape, surface coating, the volume of clinical research behind it, and how many years of long-term data exist for the system. The price difference between brands reflects exactly these factors, not an arbitrary markup.

JDental — the Affordable Option

JDental is an affordably priced system with a good success rate for typical clinical cases. It suits situations where bone volume is sufficient and no complex rehabilitation protocol is needed. A smart choice when budget is the priority factor, all else being clinically equal.

Straumann — Swiss Quality for Complex Cases

Straumann is one of the most extensively studied brands in implantology, with one of the highest long-term success rates. It's often chosen for clinically more complex situations — where the risk of rejection or complications needs to be minimized.

Nobel Biocare — Premium and Full-Mouth Rehabilitation

Nobel Biocare is a premium system that performs especially well in esthetically critical zones (front teeth) and in full-jaw rehabilitation protocols, including All-on-4 and All-on-6. The higher price reflects both the brand and the engineering behind these complex-case solutions.

How the System Is Actually Chosen in Practice

The final decision is made after CT diagnostics: the dentist assesses the volume and quality of the bone, the implant site (chewing or esthetic zone), overall oral health, and the patient's budget. Sometimes a single treatment plan uses different systems for different zones — that's normal practice, not a compromise on quality.

Considering Implants?

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Which implant is better — Nobel Biocare, Straumann, or JDental?

"Better" depends on the case and the budget: all three systems are certified and used at the clinic. Straumann and Nobel Biocare are premium options with the longest clinical track record, while JDental is an affordable option with a good success rate.

Why do implants from different systems vary so much in price?

Price depends on the country of origin, the volume of research and clinical trials behind the brand, and how long the system has been on the market.

Can I find out in advance which system will suit me?

Yes — the final choice of system is discussed at a consultation after a CT scan, where the dentist takes into account bone volume, the implant site, and your budget.
