What It Is

Cavity Treatment and Tooth Restoration

Restorative treatment covers cavity treatment at any stage and rebuilding the tooth's shape with composite material matched precisely to the color of your enamel.

Cavities

We treat cavities at any stage — from an early spot of decay to deep lesions close to the nerve.

Filling

We rebuild the tooth's shape with light-cured composite in layers, fitted precisely to your bite.

Materials

Modern composites are matched to your enamel shade — the restoration is invisible to the eye.

Visit Steps

How Treatment Works

  1. Diagnostics

    Examination and, if needed, a targeted X-ray to assess the depth of the decay.

  2. Anesthesia

    Local anesthesia so treatment is completely comfortable.

  3. Removing the Decay

    The doctor removes the affected tooth tissue while preserving as much healthy structure as possible.

  4. Filling and Polishing

    Rebuilding the tooth's shape with composite in layers, then a final polish for a natural enamel shine.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Cavity Treatment

Is cavity treatment painful?

Treatment is done under local anesthesia and is painless. Slight discomfort is possible only with deep decay close to the nerve.

How long does a filling last?

On average 5–10 years, depending on the material, the tooth's location, and chewing load — the exact timeframe is discussed at your consultation.

Can several teeth be treated in one visit?

Yes, if the scope of treatment allows it — the dentist will assess this at the examination and propose an optimal plan across visits.

What if a tooth is badly damaged by decay?

If the damage is extensive, a crown or veneer may be needed instead of a filling — the dentist will propose a solution at your consultation.
