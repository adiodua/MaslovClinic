About Our Services

Full-Cycle Dentistry in One Place

MaslovClinic on Arkadiivske Plato in Odesa offers diagnostics, hygiene, restorative and root canal treatment, orthodontics (braces and aligners), prosthodontics (crowns and veneers), oral surgery, dental implants, and sedation for anxious patients. Each area is led by a specialist in that field, and complex cases — full-mouth rehabilitation, All-on-4/6 — are planned under a single protocol from diagnostics through to the final restoration.

Dentistry in Odesa on Arkadiivske Plato

The clinic is located in the Prymorskyi district of Odesa, just 100 meters from the sea — easy to reach from the city center and Arkadia. For more about the clinic, licenses, and certificates, see the "About the Clinic" page.

Dental Implants in Odesa

We work with three implant systems — Nobel Biocare, Straumann, and JDental — to match different budgets and clinical needs, from single-tooth implants to full All-on-4/6 rehabilitation. See details and prices on the "Dental Implants" page.

Cosmetic Dentistry — Veneers and Crowns

Ceramic veneers and crowns made from individual impressions — we match shape and shade to your specific case. See the treatment steps and pricing on the "Crowns & Veneers" page.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Prices and Services

Are the prices in the price list final?

The listed prices are starting ("from") prices — for most services, the exact cost depends on the scope of work and the clinical situation. We confirm the final price after an examination and diagnostics, before treatment begins.

Can I pay for treatment in installments?

We can discuss installment options during your consultation, depending on the amount and treatment plan.

Is there a charge for the first consultation?

The cost of the initial consultation is listed under "Diagnostics" — it is credited toward your treatment plan if you continue treatment at the clinic.

How do I know which service is right for me?

Start with diagnostics and a consultation — the dentist will examine your mouth, order imaging if needed, and propose a treatment plan with exact prices.

Can I get several services in one visit?

Yes, if the clinical situation allows it — for example, hygiene and restorative treatment are often combined into a single visit.
