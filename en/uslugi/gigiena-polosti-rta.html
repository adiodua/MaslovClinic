What it is

Plaque, tartar removal, and polishing

Professional cleaning removes soft plaque and hardened tartar with ultrasound, then polishes the enamel so plaque doesn't build back up as quickly.

What's included

Ultrasonic removal of plaque and tartar, enamel polishing with paste, and Air Flow where needed.

How often you need it

The recommended frequency is once every 6 months — more often if you're prone to tartar buildup, per your doctor's recommendation.

Result

Smooth, clean tooth surfaces, a lower risk of cavities and gum inflammation, and fresh breath.

Visit stages

How the procedure works

  1. Examination

    The doctor assesses the condition of your teeth and gums and determines the amount of buildup.

  2. Plaque and tartar removal

    An ultrasonic scaler gently removes hardened and soft buildup without damaging the enamel.

  3. Polishing

    A polishing paste smooths the enamel surface, reducing its tendency to collect new plaque.

  4. Recommendations

    The doctor selects a brush, toothpaste, and brushing technique suited to your specific case.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about cleaning

How often is professional cleaning needed?

We recommend it once every 6 months to help prevent cavities and gum disease — more often if you're prone to tartar buildup.

Does tartar removal hurt?

The procedure is usually comfortable. If your gums are sensitive, the doctor can use local anesthesia.

Can I have a cleaning during pregnancy?

Yes, professional cleaning is safe at any stage of pregnancy and is especially recommended because of the increased risk of gum inflammation.

Does cleaning remove yellow staining from teeth?

Cleaning removes plaque and tartar, restoring your enamel's natural color, but it doesn't lighten it — for a whiter shade you'll need a separate whitening treatment.
