About the Clinic

Dental Care in Odesa on Arkadiivske Plato

MaslovClinic is a full-cycle dental clinic in Odesa's Prymorskyi district, just 100 meters from the sea. We bring restorative, surgical, and prosthetic treatment, dental implants, and cosmetic dentistry together under one roof — no need to travel between different specialists.

Implant Treatment in Odesa

We work with the Nobel Biocare, Straumann, and JDental implant systems — from a single implant to a full All-on-4/6 rehabilitation. Learn more on our "Implant Treatment" page.

Dental Care Near the Sea and the City Center

The clinic is located on Arkadiivske Plato, 5A, Bldg 1 — an easy trip from both Arkadia and central Odesa. Free parking is available for patients. See the full list of treatments on our "Services" page, and meet our team on the "Doctors" page.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get to MaslovClinic?+
The clinic is located in Odesa on Arkadiivske Plato, 5A, Bldg 1, between Genuezka and Posmitnoho streets, 100 meters from the sea. It's just as easy to reach from central Odesa as from the Prymorskyi district.
Does the clinic have free parking?+
Yes — MaslovClinic offers free parking for patients, so there's no need to hunt for a spot in advance or pay for parking separately.
Dental care in Odesa's Prymorskyi district — why choose Arkadiivske Plato?+
Arkadiivske Plato is one of Odesa's most well-developed areas — green, and right by the sea. Patients from the Prymorskyi district and the city center can get here just as easily as local residents.
How much does a dental implant cost in Odesa?+
The cost depends on the implant system, the amount of available bone, whether bone grafting is needed, and the type of crown. We give you an exact price after a consultation and diagnostic workup — you'll receive a treatment plan with a full cost breakdown before any treatment begins.
How do Nobel Biocare implants differ from cheaper alternatives?+
Nobel Biocare is a premium system backed by a long history of clinical research, precise thread geometry, and predictable osseointegration. This affects both how long the implant lasts and the outcome of prosthetic work in complex cases.
What is full-mouth rehabilitation, and who is it for?+
Full-mouth rehabilitation is a comprehensive restoration of all teeth when multiple problems are present — combining implants, prosthetics, and a bite correction within a single treatment plan. It's suited to significant tooth loss or dental care that's been put off for a long time.
All-on-6 or All-on-4 — which implant protocol should I choose?+
The choice depends on the amount of available bone and the load the future prosthesis will bear. All-on-6 spreads the load across more implants and suits lower bone density, while All-on-4 is a more compact protocol. The decision is made after 3D diagnostic imaging.
How long do ceramic crowns last?+
With proper care and regular check-ups, ceramic restorations last for many years. Lifespan depends on oral hygiene, chewing load, and the crown material.
Which should I choose — veneers or crowns?+
Veneers preserve more of your natural tooth structure and work well for cosmetic correction of shape and color. Crowns are needed when a tooth is severely damaged or has had root canal treatment. We give a precise recommendation at an in-person consultation.
What happens at the first consultation before implant treatment?+
An exam, 3D bone diagnostics, a discussion of treatment protocol options, and a transparent cost breakdown — no surprises at later visits.
Is sedation used during treatment?+
Yes, when clinically indicated — for long or complex procedures such as full-mouth rehabilitation. The decision to use sedation is made on a case-by-case basis.
How many visits does full-mouth rehabilitation take?+
The number of visits is individual and depends on the scope of work — anywhere from a few appointments to a protocol spread over several months if implant healing time is needed. A plan with stages and timelines is drawn up in advance.
How are sterilization and diagnostics organized at the clinic?+
The clinic has a dedicated sterilization room with Tecno-Gaz autoclaves and a separate room for 3D X-ray/CBCT diagnostics — this allows for precise treatment planning and strict safety protocols.
Do you offer a warranty on implants and restorations?+
Yes — warranty terms are discussed at the consultation and depend on the type of treatment and the materials or implant systems used.
